- Collections: `addix` (PEAKS, Randonnée, BiCYCLE CLUB, EVEN magazines, with photos), `chronicle` (Japanese business podcast with per-sentence audio cues), `mugenlabo` (KDDI startup magazine), `telegraph-tokyo-2026` (English).
- `preview` is free; `confirm` unlocks full text and spends team credits (~$0.20-0.90 per result). Always preview first and unlock only what you need.

- Content is Japanese: search in Japanese. Cite publisher, title, date (and issue/page or episode date).
- Licensed for this hackathon: show short excerpts with attribution, never commit full articles, transcripts or cached results.
